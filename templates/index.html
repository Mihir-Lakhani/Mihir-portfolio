About My Work

My machine-learning journey began with a decision-tree cardiovascular project built without machine-learning libraries, which made the fundamentals tangible. Later, as an AI/ML Research Intern on a Network Digital Twin Project, I explored 6G-oriented network digital twins, SLA-breach prediction, explainability, and proactive network-control ideas. That work led to the Closed Loop SLA Violation Simulation, presented at Nokia Campus Connect 2025, and helped shape the Fleet Smart Vehicle Digital Twin Prototype. Alongside these projects, I built a Source-Cited RAG Assistant that lets visitors explore approved public portfolio material with validated citations and approved diagrams.
